# Amazon US Seller Registration

## Purpose

Help a real Chinese company complete Amazon US registration safely and consistently. Distinguish guidance from live operation, preserve the user's chosen ZiNiao environment, and stop before identity, legal, financial, or irreversible decisions.

Read [references/registration-playbook.md](references/registration-playbook.md) before advising on fields, troubleshooting a blocker, or operating a live registration.

## Establish the current phase

Inspect the visible page or ask only for the missing fact needed to identify one phase:

1. planning and historical-account check;
2. document and master-data preparation;
3. ZiNiao environment setup;
4. Amazon account and company registration;
5. charge card setup;
6. WorldFirst enterprise account or USD deposit method;
7. identity, address, or bank verification;
8. W-8BEN-E tax interview;
9. account acceptance, permissions, return settings, or FBA readiness.

Do not restart registration merely because a page is confusing. Continue from the current valid account and preserve its records.

## Work in this order

1. Check for existing or suspended Amazon seller accounts involving the company, legal representative, email, phone, card, or receiving account.
2. Build one source-of-truth table from the business license, identity document, address proof, card issuer record, and WorldFirst record.
3. Create one ZiNiao account environment for the store and bind its intended device/network.
4. Keep one registration operator in that environment during review.
5. Enter company, representative, beneficial-owner, charge-card, deposit-account, store-survey, and plan information from the source-of-truth table.
6. Upload only current, unedited documents that belong to the exact registered entity or person.
7. Let the named person complete face/video verification.
8. Bind the WorldFirst US USD account to Amazon.com and obtain an exact-match account proof when requested.
9. Complete the tax interview only after resolving entity classification and authorized signer.
10. Verify that critical restrictions are cleared before calling the account usable.
11. Configure individual Amazon users, return settings, product compliance, and FBA separately.

## Use live UI tools carefully

When the user asks to operate ZiNiao, a browser, or a local app:

1. Announce that the computer-use skill is being used and why.
2. Read the full `computer-use:computer-use` skill before acting.
3. Inspect fresh UI state before every action and again after every navigation or submission.
4. Prefer visible labels and accessibility data; never guess coordinates from an old screenshot.
5. Do not move the Amazon master account into a normal browser or a different ZiNiao environment merely for convenience.
6. Hand control to the user for passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHA, full card data, CVV, face/video capture, and any secret that would be exposed by typing or narration.
7. For a traveling legal representative, navigate to but do not start the verification session, test camera/microphone in the same approved environment, then hand control to the named person. Do not bypass a device or location check.
8. If the current page contradicts the user's company facts, stop instead of forcing a selection.

## Mandatory stop points

Stop immediately before any of these actions and explain the exact decision or consequence:

- selecting or changing the legal entity, legal representative, beneficial owner, or historical-account strategy;
- creating a new Amazon master account, accepting seller terms, or choosing a plan that creates a recurring fee;
- creating an additional Amazon seller account;
- uploading identity, business-license, address, or banking documents unless the user has explicitly approved that exact file and destination; obtain a new approval for a different file or destination;
- starting face, selfie, liveness, or video verification;
- entering, replacing, or submitting a full card or bank account;
- accepting a paid ZiNiao plan or any charge;
- signing or finally submitting W-8BEN-E, tax certifications, binding declarations, or platform terms;
- closing an account, deleting a payment method, submitting an appeal, or acknowledging a violation;
- making a tax classification where the facts do not fit the ordinary-company path in the reference.

Treat a prior general instruction such as “continue registration” as authorization for routine navigation and form preparation, not as approval for a new legal signature, biometric check, payment, or materially different entity choice.

## Protect sensitive data

- Never repeat full identity numbers, card numbers, account numbers, passwords, OTPs, CVVs, recovery codes, or residential addresses in commentary, filenames, manuals, or reusable skill files.
- Use placeholders and last four digits only when tracking status.
- Do not edit, enhance, recreate, or manufacture KYC documents or bank statements.
- Keep downloaded official PDFs unchanged.
- Avoid screenshots containing secrets; crop or redact only for communication, never alter the submission document.
- Store master credentials and recovery codes in the user's password manager, not in ZiNiao notes or chat.

## Verify volatile or high-stakes claims

Browse current official Amazon, WorldFirst, ZiNiao, and IRS sources before stating current fees, accepted card types, required documents, marketplace structure, tax form behavior, or a provider workflow. Prefer the exact live page over remembered wording. Mark tax guidance as general information, not professional tax advice.

## Apply these fixed distinctions

- `Charge Method` is Amazon's card for fees. It is not the payout account.
- `Deposit Method` is the bank or payment-service account receiving sales proceeds.
- A WorldFirst Chinese beneficiary/SWIFT screen configures withdrawal from WorldFirst to a local bank; it does not create the Amazon USD receiving account.
- For a WorldFirst US USD account, the Amazon deposit method normally uses bank location `United States` and the provider's routing/account details.
- A North America account may display several marketplaces automatically. That does not mean the seller must operate or pay plan fees separately in all of them. Assign and operate only the intended marketplace.
- Registration survey answers about category, brand, and UPC describe the current plan; they do not permanently lock the catalog. Keep them truthful.
- A healthy account is not automatically FBA-ready. Deposit, charge, tax, restrictions, returns, listing, GTIN/brand, compliance, and FBA enrollment remain separate gates.

## Completion criteria

Call registration complete only when all applicable checks pass:

- Seller Central login works and the intended US marketplace opens;
- company and representative data are accurate;
- identity and address checks are passed or not required;
- deposit method is verified and assigned to Amazon.com;
- charge method is valid;
- tax interview is accepted;
- no critical, limited-access, re-verification, or registration task remains;
- account ownership, email, MFA, recovery, ZiNiao environment, and records are under company control.

Describe FBA readiness separately. Do not claim the seller can ship inventory until listing, category/product compliance, identifiers, FBA enrollment, labels, and shipping-plan requirements are satisfied.
